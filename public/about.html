ℹ️ About Future's Relic Rewards

Connecting NFT holders with exclusive rewards on the WAX blockchain

🎯 What is FR Rewards?

Future's Relic Rewards is a community-focused platform that enables NFT holders to claim exclusive rewards based on their collection holdings. Built on the WAX blockchain, our platform provides a seamless and secure way to distribute rewards to loyal collectors.

Whether you're a collector, project owner, or community member, FR Rewards makes it easy to manage and distribute NFT-based rewards with customizable cooldowns, multi-template support, and comprehensive tracking.

🚀 How It Works

  • Connect Your Wallet: Link your WAX wallet to verify your NFT holdings
  • Check Eligibility: The system automatically detects qualifying NFTs in your wallet
  • Claim Rewards: Click to claim available rewards based on your holdings
  • Track History: View your claim history and next available claim times
  • Explore Stories: Dive into the lore and discover the world of Future's Relic

🌐 Technology Stack

FR Rewards is built with modern web technologies:

  • Blockchain: WAX (Worldwide Asset eXchange)
  • Smart Contracts: AtomicAssets for NFT management
  • Wallet Integration: Cloud Wallet, Anchor, Wombat, and more
  • Backend: Node.js with Express
  • Database: SQLite with Better-SQLite3
  • Frontend: Modern vanilla JavaScript with responsive design
