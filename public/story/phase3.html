The First Contact

"It started with this Telegram. No return address. Just a message stamped 'INSTANT. ENCRYPTED. WORLDWIDE.'

'FILM SET REMAINS FOUND IN DESERT... DONATING FINDS TO YOUR LABS.'

The sender signed only as A Curious Traveller. Days later, this Postal Card arrived, confirming three distinct filming locations buried in the dunes. He mentioned Customs might be a problem. He was right. But who is he?"
