**Welcome!** This guide will help you get the most out of the Future's Relic rewards system.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### What is this?

The Future's Relic Rewards System lets you:
- **Claim free NFTs** by holding specific Editor Cards
- **Craft new NFTs** by combining existing ones in the Factory
- **Follow story paths** with interactive narrative experiences
- **Earn rewards** for being part of the community

### What do I need?

- A **WAX wallet** (like Anchor or WAX Cloud Wallet)
- At least one **Future's Relic NFT** (Editor Cards, etc.)
- That's it! No coding or technical knowledge required

## 🎁 Claiming Rewards

If you own specific Future's Relic NFTs (like Editor Cards), you can claim **free reward NFTs** regularly!

- ✅ **Eligible for**: Wax Seal

- ⏰ **Cooldown**: 24 hours
- 🟢 **Status**: Ready to claim!

Have multiple rewards ready? Click **"🎁 Claim All Available Rewards"** to get them all at once!

## ⏰ Understanding Cooldowns

### What's a Cooldown?

A **cooldown** is a waiting period between claims. It keeps the system fair and prevents spam.

**Example**:
- You claim a Wax Seal at 3:00 PM
- Cooldown is 24 hours
- You can claim again at 3:00 PM tomorrow

### Reading the Timer

The page shows a **countdown** for each reward:
- `23h 45m 12s` = 23 hours, 45 minutes, 12 seconds remaining
- ✅ **"Ready to claim!"** = Cooldown finished, claim now!
- ⏰ **Timer counting down** = Wait for it to reach zero

### Multiple NFTs

If you own multiple eligible NFTs:
- Each one has its own cooldown
- Claiming from one doesn't affect the others
- You can claim from all of them when ready!

## 🏭 Using the Factory (Crafting)

### What is the Factory?

The **Factory** lets you combine NFTs to create new ones! Think of it like crafting in a video game.

### How to Craft

**Step 1: Browse Recipes**
- Go to the **Factory** page
- See all available crafting recipes
- Example: "4x Wax Seals → 1x crEDIT Card"

**Step 2: Check Requirements**
- Make sure you own the required ingredients
- The page shows: "You have 5 Wax Seals (need 4)" ✅
- Red text means you don't have enough ❌

**Step 3: Select Your NFTs**
- Click a recipe to start crafting
- Choose which specific NFTs to use as ingredients
- You can pick based on mint number, rarity, etc.

**Step 4: Transfer & Craft**
- Click **"Craft"** button
- Your wallet will ask you to approve transferring the ingredients
- Approve the transaction
- The system verifies and processes your craft
- You receive your new NFT! 🎉

### Mint vs Pool/Swap Mode

Some recipes have **two modes**:

**🔨 Mint Mode** (Default):
- Creates a **brand new NFT** from scratch
- Uses **more ingredients** (e.g., 4x Wax Seals)
- Always available
- Your ingredients are burned/destroyed

**🔄 Pool/Swap Mode** (Special):
- Swaps for an **existing NFT** from the community pool
- Uses **fewer ingredients** (e.g., 3x Wax Seals instead of 4)
- Only available if the pool has inventory
- **Cheaper and more eco-friendly!**

The page tells you which modes are available:
- ✅ **Green "Swap" button** = Pool has inventory (cheaper option!)
- 🔨 **Blue "Mint" button** = Create new NFT

### Batch Crafting

Save time and transaction fees by crafting multiple at once:
- Instead of crafting 1x at a time
- You can craft **2x, 3x, 5x**, etc. in one transaction
- Example: "Craft 3x" uses 12 Wax Seals → Get 3 crEDIT Cards

### What if Something Goes Wrong?

Crafts rarely fail, but if they do:
- Your ingredients are held **safely** in the collection wallet
- The admin can either:
  - **Fulfill the craft** (send you your result NFT)
  - **Refund your ingredients** (send them back to you)
- You **won't lose your NFTs**!

### What is the Story System?

The **Story Workflow** is an interactive narrative experience where you:
- Follow branching story paths
- Make choices that affect the outcome
- Unlock new chapters and content
- Use your NFTs to access special paths
- Earn rewards for completing stories

### Getting Started with Stories

1. **Visit the Story page** - Look for "Story" or "Narrative" link
2. **Connect your wallet** - Same as claiming rewards
3. **View available stories** - See which paths you can access
4. **Start reading!** - Click on a story to begin

### How Stories Work

**📑 Tabs** = Different chapters or story sections
- Like chapters in a book
- Some tabs are locked until you complete previous ones
- Unlock by finishing requirements

**📖 Steps** = Individual story moments
- Each step has text, images, or videos
- Read the narrative
- Make choices when prompted

**⚡ Actions** = Things you can do
- **Read**: Just enjoy the narrative
- **Choose**: Pick between options (affects story outcome)
- **Collect**: Claim rewards for completing sections
- **Craft**: Some paths require crafting specific NFTs

### Example Story Flow

```
📖 Chapter 1: The Beginning
```

### Requirements & Conditions

The system shows you:

### Story Progress

Your progress is **saved automatically**:

### Making Choices

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Claiming Rewards

**Q: I don't see any rewards. Why?**
- Make sure you own Future's Relic NFTs (Editor Cards, etc.)
- Connect your wallet first
- Check if you're on cooldown (countdown timer showing)
- Only specific NFTs are eligible for rewards

**Q: The claim button is disabled. What do I do?**
- Check the countdown timer - you might be on cooldown
- Make sure you still own the required NFT
- Try refreshing the page
- Wait for the timer to reach zero

**Q: I claimed but didn't get the NFT!**
- Wait a few seconds - blockchain confirmation takes time
- Check your AtomicHub inventory
- Look for the transaction ID link to verify on blockchain
- If truly missing after 2 minutes, contact admin with transaction ID

**Q: Can I claim multiple times per day?**
- Depends on the cooldown period
- Most rewards have 24-hour cooldowns
- Some may be longer or shorter
- Each NFT template has its own cooldown

**Q: Do I lose my NFTs when I claim?**
- **No!** Your original NFTs stay in your wallet
- Only the *reward* NFT is newly minted to you
- You keep all your NFTs!

### Factory Crafting

**Q: What's the difference between Mint and Pool/Swap?**
- **Mint**: Creates a brand new NFT (uses more ingredients)
- **Pool/Swap**: Trades for existing NFT from pool (uses fewer ingredients)
- Pool/Swap is **cheaper** when available!
- Pool/Swap only appears if the pool has inventory

**Q: My craft failed. Did I lose my NFTs?**
- **No!** Your NFTs are held safely in the collection wallet
- The admin can fulfill the craft (send you your result)
- Or the admin can refund your ingredients
- Failed crafts are tracked and resolved - you won't lose anything

**Q: Can I cancel a craft after transferring?**
- No, once you approve the transaction, it's on the blockchain
- However, if it fails, you can get a refund (see above)
- Always double-check before approving!

**Q: How do I know which NFTs to select?**
- Any NFTs matching the required template ID will work
- You can choose based on mint number, rarity, personal preference
- **Lower mints don't give better results** - all work the same!
- Keep your favorites, use the others!

**Q: What happens to ingredients after crafting?**
- **Mint mode**: Ingredients are burned/destroyed
- **Pool/Swap mode**: Ingredients go to the collection pool
- This is normal and expected!
- You get your new NFT in return

### Story System

**Q: How do I unlock new story chapters?**
- Complete previous chapters first (in order)
- Meet any NFT requirements (own specific cards)
- Complete required crafting tasks if needed
- Some chapters may be time-gated

**Q: Can I go back and change my choices?**
- Usually **no** - story choices are permanent
- This makes your journey unique!
- Choose carefully and enjoy your path
- Some stories may allow replays in the future

**Q: I'm stuck - how do I proceed?**
- Check the requirements panel (shows what's needed)
- Make sure you have required NFTs in your wallet
- Complete any needed crafts first
- Some sections may be time-gated (come back later)
- Refresh the page if it seems stuck

**Q: Do I lose NFTs by participating in stories?**
- **No!** Stories just **check** if you own NFTs
- Your NFTs stay safely in your wallet
- Unless a story specifically includes a craft (which is optional)
- You're always in control of your NFTs

**Q: Can I do stories on mobile?**
- Yes! The story system works on mobile browsers
- Use mobile wallet apps (Anchor mobile, WAX Cloud Wallet)
- Best viewed in landscape mode for better reading
- Touch-friendly interface

### Technical Issues

**Q: The page won't load. What do I do?**
- Refresh the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Brave)
- Check your internet connection
- Try incognito/private mode

**Q: My wallet won't connect.**
- Make sure you have WAX Cloud Wallet or Anchor installed
- Check if your wallet extension is enabled in browser
- Try logging out of your wallet and back in
- Update your wallet software to latest version
- Try the other wallet option

**Q: The countdown timer seems wrong.**
- Timer shows **your local time** (your timezone)
- Refresh the page to sync
- Check your device's clock settings
- Time zones may make it seem different

**Q: Transaction failed or is stuck.**
- Check your **CPU/NET resources** in your wallet
- You may need to stake more WAX
- Wait a few minutes and try again
- If problem persists, contact admin
- Provide transaction ID if you have one

**Q: Images or videos won't load.**
- Images may take time to load from IPFS
- Wait 30 seconds and try refreshing
- Check your internet connection
- Some content may be large files
- Try a different browser if issues persist
