# User Guide - WAX NFT Rewards System

Complete guide for users to claim NFT rewards.

## What is This?

This is a rewards system for NFT holders. If you own specific NFTs from our collection, you can claim reward NFTs on a regular cooldown basis (typically every 24 hours).

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Visit the Website

Navigate to the rewards website URL provided by the administrator.

### Step 2: Connect Your Wallet

You'll see two wallet options:

#### Option A: Wax Cloud Wallet (WCW)

1. Click "Wax Cloud Wallet" button
2. A popup will appear
3. Login to your Wax Cloud Wallet
4. Approve the connection
5. You're connected!

#### Option B: Anchor Wallet

1. Click "Anchor Wallet" button
2. Anchor will open (desktop app or mobile)
3. Approve the connection request
4. Select which account to use
5. You're connected!

### Step 3: Check Your Eligibility

Once connected, the system will automatically:
- Check your wallet for NFTs
- Verify if you hold eligible templates
- Display your eligibility status

## Understanding Your Status

### ✅ You Are Eligible

If you see this message:
- You hold at least one whitelisted NFT
- You can claim rewards!
- Scroll down to see your NFTs and claim status

### ❌ No Eligible NFTs Found

If you see this message:
- You don't currently hold any whitelisted NFTs
- The page will show which templates are eligible
- Click "View on NeftyBlocks" to purchase eligible NFTs

## Claiming Rewards

### When You Can Claim

Each eligible NFT you own has its own cooldown timer:

**Ready to Claim** (✅):
- Green "Claim Reward" button is active
- Click to claim your reward!

**Cooldown Active** (⏰):
- Timer shows remaining time (e.g., "23h 45m 12s")
- Button is disabled
- Wait for timer to reach zero

### How to Claim

1. Find an NFT with "Ready to claim!" status
2. Click the green "🎁 Claim Reward" button
3. Your wallet will prompt you to approve the transaction
4. Approve the transaction
5. Wait a few seconds for confirmation
6. Success! Your reward NFT is minted to your wallet
7. Cooldown timer resets

### What Happens When You Claim

- A new NFT is minted directly to your wallet
- The transaction is recorded on the WAX blockchain
- Your cooldown timer starts for that template
- You can view the transaction on a block explorer

## Claim History

Below your NFTs, you'll see your recent claims:
- Date and time of each claim
- Which template was used to claim
- Last 5 claims are shown

## Multiple NFTs

If you own multiple eligible NFTs:
- Each one has its own cooldown timer
- You can claim from each one independently
- Timers don't affect each other

**Example:**
- You own Template #217679 and Template #217680
- You can claim from both (if cooldowns are ready)
- Claiming from one doesn't affect the other's cooldown

## Cooldown System

### How It Works

- Each template has a cooldown period (usually 24 hours)
- After claiming, you must wait for the cooldown to expire
- Timer counts down in real-time
- When it reaches zero, you can claim again

### Timer Format

`23h 45m 12s` means:
- 23 hours
- 45 minutes
- 12 seconds remaining

## Tips & Best Practices

### Maximize Your Rewards

1. **Check Daily**: Login every 24 hours to claim
2. **Own Multiple NFTs**: Each eligible NFT can claim independently
3. **Set Reminders**: Note when your cooldown expires
4. **Stay Connected**: Keep your wallet session active

### Troubleshooting

**Wallet Won't Connect**
- Try refreshing the page
- Make sure wallet extension/app is installed
- Try the other wallet option
- Clear browser cache

**Not Showing as Eligible**
- Wait a few seconds for the blockchain to update
- Refresh the page
- Verify you own the NFT on AtomicHub
- Check you're connected with the correct account

**Claim Button Disabled**
- Check the countdown timer
- Wait for cooldown to expire
- Refresh page if timer reached zero

**Transaction Failed**
- Check your CPU/NET resources on WAX
- Try again in a few moments
- Ensure you have enough resources staked

**Reward Not Received**
- Check AtomicHub inventory (may take 30-60 seconds)
- Look for the transaction ID in the success message
- View on block explorer to verify

## Disconnecting

To disconnect your wallet:
1. Click "Disconnect" button in top right
2. Your session will end
3. You can reconnect anytime

## Security

### Safe Practices

- Never share your private keys
- Only connect on the official website
- Verify the URL before connecting
- The system NEVER asks for private keys

### What the System Can Do

- Read your NFT holdings (public blockchain data)
- Send transactions you approve
- Mint NFTs to your account (with your approval)

### What the System Cannot Do

- Access your private keys
- Transfer your existing NFTs
- Make transactions without your approval

## FAQs

**Q: How often can I claim?**
A: Typically every 24 hours per eligible NFT template.

**Q: Do I need to pay anything?**
A: Only minimal WAX blockchain fees (CPU/NET). The rewards are free!

**Q: Can I claim if I buy an eligible NFT?**
A: Yes! As soon as you own an eligible NFT, you can claim (if cooldown allows).

**Q: What if I sell my NFT after claiming?**
A: You keep the reward, but can't claim again without owning the NFT.

**Q: Can I claim from multiple NFTs at once?**
A: No, each claim is a separate transaction. But you can claim multiple times in succession.

**Q: Is there a limit to total claims?**
A: Usually no limit, as long as you own eligible NFTs and wait for cooldowns.

**Q: What blockchain is this on?**
A: WAX blockchain (Worldwide Asset eXchange).

**Q: Where can I see my rewards?**
A: Check your AtomicHub inventory or any WAX NFT marketplace.

**Q: Can I use a mobile wallet?**
A: Yes! Both Wax Cloud Wallet and Anchor work on mobile.
